--- layout: home author_profile: true title: "About" --- Chen-Chou Lo (羅振洲) is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Yuan Ze University, starting August 2026. He received his Ph.D. from KU Leuven in 2025, where his research focused on radar-camera fusion and depth estimation, supervised by Patrick Vandewalle. Prior to his Ph.D., he was a research assistant at Academia Sinica, working on speech processing research, and before that a senior software engineer at ASUS, where he worked on SLAM and robotics systems. His research interests span computer vision, speech processing, and multimodal AI, with a particular interest in problems that combine perception across modalities. ## Education * Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering, KU Leuven, 2025 * M.S., National Central University (NCU) * B.S. in Electrical Engineering, Chang Gung University ## Experience * Assistant Professor, Yuan Ze University, Dept. of EE (2026–) * Ph.D. Researcher, KU Leuven (2019–2025) * Research Assistant, Academia Sinica (2018–2019) * Senior Software Engineer (SLAM/Robotics), ASUS (2015–2018)
