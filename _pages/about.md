**Chen-Chou Lo (羅振洲)** is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Yuan Ze University, starting August 2026. He received his Ph.D. from KU Leuven in 2025, where his research focused on radar-camera fusion and depth estimation, supervised by Patrick Vandewalle. Following his Ph.D., he worked as a postdoctoral researcher at Academia Sinica before joining Yuan Ze University.

Prior to his Ph.D., he was a research assistant at Academia Sinica, working on speech processing research, and before that a senior software engineer at ASUS.

His research interests span computer vision, speech processing, and multimodal AI, with a particular interest in problems that combine perception across modalities.

## Education

* Ph.D. in Engineering Technology, KU Leuven, Belgium, *2019 – 2025*
* M.S. in Electrical Engineering, National Central University, Taiwan, *2011 – 2014*
* B.S. in Electrical Engineering, Chang Gung University, Taiwan, *2007 – 2011*
